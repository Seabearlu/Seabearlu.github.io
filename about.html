About

Independent research for practical software decisions.

Bear Lu Research is an independent technology publication focused on SaaS, artificial intelligence, marketing technology, analytics, and developer platforms.

The goal is simple: translate complex product capabilities into practical guidance for startups and small businesses. Coverage emphasizes implementation effort, data flows, integration quality, operating costs, and measurable business outcomes.

Technical perspective

The publication is informed by experience with software development, web APIs, analytics, conversion tracking, and affiliate-platform integrations. This technical perspective helps separate a polished feature page from what a product can realistically deliver inside a working stack.

Editorial principles

  • Explain who a product is—and is not—for.
  • Evaluate workflows and constraints, not marketing claims alone.
  • Disclose commercial relationships clearly.
  • Never allow compensation to guarantee a positive conclusion.

Publisher

Bear Lu is an independent technical publisher and growth practitioner researching software used by startups, small businesses, marketers, and developers.
